Membership

Membership in the Society of the Cincinnati in the State of Rhode Island and Providence Plantations is reserved for those qualified male descendants of commissioned officers who served in the Continental Army or Navy, or of officers of the French royal forces who served in America during the Revolutionary War in Rhode Island.

While there are currently just over 100 members of the Rhode Island Society there are still eligible officers available.

Please contact James Longley at jamesplongley@gmail.com if you believe you may qualify.
